## Scene 1 — The White Road (16 seconds)

Purpose: establish remembered Bangalore, direction, parent/child relationship and
the defining counterflow image.

| Cut | Duration | Camera | Visual beat |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 1. The road before Abhishek | 3s | Extreme wide, eye-level, locked | Wind moves enormous trees above the pale road before Abhishek enters. |
| 2. Abhishek enters the frame | 3s | Very wide, locked | He enters frame-left and runs right with the quiet toddler in the chest carrier. |
| 3. Running against traffic | 4s | Wide, slightly low, slow lateral track | Hero frame: Abhishek and child move right while autos move left. |
| 4. The quiet passenger | 3s | Full-body profile tracking | Establish the carrier silhouette and the child's quietness. |
| 5. No sound from the child | 3s | Medium close, stable tracking | Abhishek's anxiety and the infant's calm share the same frame. |

Continuity: pale rolled-sleeve shirt, charcoal trousers, dark shoes; fictional
toddler in a light one-piece; charcoal canvas carrier; screen direction right.

## Scene 3 — Nine O'Clock (16 seconds)

Purpose: reveal the destination and externalize the unexplained deadline. The
evening-to-morning transition is intentional dream logic.

| Cut | Duration | Camera | Visual beat |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 10. Arches through the trees | 3s | Long establishing view, gentle push | Stone arches and clock tower first appear through moving branches. |
| 11. Arrival at the old school | 4s | Extreme wide, locked | The full dream-school appears; evening has abruptly become morning. |
| 12. Exactly nine | 2s | Straight-on extreme close insert | The tower clock reads exactly 9:00 AM. |
| 13. Checking the deadline | 3s | Profile medium, subtle push-in | Abhishek and the clock share one composition and eyeline. |
| 14. Running under the arches | 4s | Rear three-quarter full-body track | Repeating arches pull him toward the entrance. |

Location: a fictional old-stone school inspired by the dreamer's memory of
Bishop Cotton's, with no real logo or claim of surveyed architecture.

## Scene 4 — The Door Is for You (15 seconds)

Purpose: express the reveal entirely through a controlled visual transformation.

| Cut | Duration | Camera | Visual beat |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 15. Before the doorway | 3s | Straight-on full-body locked frame | Adult clothes, toddler and carrier are all clearly present. |
| 16. Last view of the child | 2s | Locked detail close-up | Confirm infant scale, quiet state and both carrier straps. |
| 17. Crossing the line | 3s | Return to exact locked full-body frame | Abhishek crosses the threshold with child and carrier still present. |
| 18. After the doorway | 3s | Exact matched frame | Same identity, pose and geometry; school uniform replaces adult clothes and child/carrier vanish. |
| 19. The school was for Abhishek | 4s | Medium three-quarter, slow push | He looks at his empty chest and around in confusion; no explanation appears. |

Transformation lock: same lens, camera coordinates, doorway, Abhishek body scale
and pose between cuts 17 and 18. No glow, particles or fantasy effect.

## Scene 5 — The Scene Collapses (10 seconds)

Purpose: move from confusion to the transcript's abrupt collapse without adding
a waking scene or turning the dream into horror.

| Cut | Duration | Camera | Visual beat |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 20. The school grows too large | 3s | Wide, slow pull-back | Realistic architecture becomes subtly oversized around Abhishek. |
| 21. The frame loses alignment | 3s | Locked with optical layer drift | Arches, clock geometry and film grain separate while Abhishek stays coherent. |
| 22. Collapse | 4s | Wide dissolving into abstraction | Stone space folds and monochrome emulsion runs out; Abhishek is last recognizable, then grey. |

## Approved design direction for review

- Working title: **Nine O'Clock**.
- Silver-memory monochrome 35mm realism.
- Grounded, tactile old Bangalore; dream logic enters through continuity and edit.
- Anxious, uncanny and humane, never horror.
- Abhishek identity comes from the supplied photo; exclude phone, mirror, room,
  selfie composition and modern black outerwear.
- Running wardrobe: pale rolled-sleeve cotton shirt, charcoal straight trousers,
  simple dark shoes.
- School state: white shirt, narrow dark tie, charcoal trousers, black shoes; no crest.
- Fictional toddler and fictional school analogue.
- No generated image receives automatic visual approval; the user reviews each.
