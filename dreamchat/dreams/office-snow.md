# Snow in the office
Source: written for testing (26 Sep 2026), not a DreamBank report: a dream shaped to exercise a soft watercolour look, weather indoors, a crowd of colleagues who do not react, a person who changes into something else, and a place that slowly transforms.

It was all "soft and watery, like a watercolour painting, the colours running into each other". I was at my
desk in the open-plan office where I work, on a normal grey afternoon, and it started snowing inside. Big
slow flakes came down from the ceiling lights, and nobody else looked up; everyone kept typing.

The snow built up on the desks and keyboards and on the carpet until the whole office was white, with only
the tops of the computer screens showing. My manager, Dele, "a tall man in a blue suit", walked over to tell
me something, and while he was talking he turned into a snowman, still in the blue suit, still holding his
coffee mug. I wasn't scared, it felt peaceful. I made a snowball and it glowed a little in my hand, and then
I woke up.
