# The jellyfish over the city
Source: written for testing (26 Sep 2026), not a DreamBank report: a dream shaped to exercise a bright anime look, a rooftop at sunset, a stranger with a bicycle, a giant creature in the sky, riding through the air, and a city seen from above.

It looked "like an anime, sharp lines and really saturated colours, that orange sunset sky". I was on the
roof of my apartment block in a big city, and there was a girl I didn't know, "short blue hair, a yellow
raincoat", with an old red bicycle. She pointed up and there was a giant jellyfish floating slowly over the
city, "pink and see-through, as big as a stadium", its long tentacles hanging down between the buildings.

She said "get on" and I sat on the back of her bicycle and she rode straight off the edge of the roof, and
instead of falling we rode through the air, up towards the jellyfish, with the whole city below us, all the
lights coming on. We rode right up into it, inside the pink light, and then I woke up.
