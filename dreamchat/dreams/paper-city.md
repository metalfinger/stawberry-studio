# The paper city
Source: written for testing (26 Sep 2026), not a DreamBank report: a dream shaped to exercise a flat, bright cut-paper look, a crowd, many small things falling, a person the dreamer knows, and a whole place that folds away.

Everything in the dream looked "flat, like it was cut out of coloured paper", bright reds and yellows and
blues, with no shading at all. I was walking through a city made of paper, with paper houses leaning over a
narrow street and "people everywhere, flat like cardboard cutouts", all going the same way.

My mum was there at a corner, folding something very carefully. She gave it to me and it was a little red
paper bird. Then it started to rain, but the rain was "hundreds of tiny paper birds" coming down, all
different colours, landing on the street and on people's shoulders.

Then the whole city started folding up around us, the houses folding down flat one after another like a
pop-up book closing, until it was just me and my mum standing on a big sheet of white paper. That's when I woke.
