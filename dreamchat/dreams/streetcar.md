# The streetcar that became a train
Source: Hall/Van de Castle norms, a woman's dream (DreamBank, series hall_female)

I was riding on a streetcar, watching the conductor operate the car. I began to notice "the lever
which he turned when we rounded a bend". It soon felt as though I were operating the car with
him: when we came to a sharp corner, I went through the motions of turning the lever and steering
the car. "The next thing I knew, I was on top of the car which had now turned into a train." It
was very high up and "the train was traveling very fast". Several times I nearly fell off, and I
was very frightened; I was afraid I would try to jump off, most of all when the train rounded a
bend. Then I noticed "another family was sitting on the top of the train with me". "A small baby
was there and seemed to be thrilled with the ride." I felt as though I was the only one who was
frightened. The train was on a country road.
