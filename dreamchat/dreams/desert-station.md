# The desert station at noon
Source: written for testing (26 Sep 2026), not a DreamBank report: a dream shaped to exercise hard noon light with black shadows in a comic-book look, a stranger and a large animal, a clock that melts, and a train that never arrives.

It looked "like a comic book, thick black lines and flat colours", and the sun was straight overhead so
every shadow was a hard black shape. I was standing on the platform of "a tiny train station in the middle of
a desert", just one bench and a clock on a pole. The sand went on forever in every direction.

An old man in a white suit was sitting on the bench with a camel standing next to him, completely calm. He
told me the train was late, like it always was. I looked at the clock and it was melting, "dripping down the
pole like wax".

I heard a train whistle far away but no train came. The old man and the camel just got up and walked off
into the desert, and I stayed on the platform watching them get smaller. Then I woke up.
