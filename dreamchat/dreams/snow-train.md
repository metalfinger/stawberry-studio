# The snow train and the red door
Source: written for testing (26 Sep 2026), not a DreamBank report: a dream shaped to exercise a moving vehicle seen from inside and outside, an old man and a suitcase that opens, weather, a stop in an empty landscape, and a door standing on its own.

I was on an old train going through snow at night, sitting across from my grandfather, who died years ago.
He had "a brown leather suitcase" on his knees. Outside the window everything was white and blue, and the
train was very quiet. He opened the suitcase and it was full of "letters, hundreds of them, all addressed to
me". He didn't say anything, he just held it out so I could see.

Then the train stopped in the middle of a snowy field. We got off together and walked through deep snow
towards "a red door standing on its own", no house around it, just the door in its frame. My grandfather
gave me the suitcase and nodded at the door. I opened it and there was warm yellow light on the other side.
That's where it ended.
