# The market on the moon
Source: written for testing (26 Sep 2026), not a DreamBank report: a dream shaped to exercise bright neon colour on a grey moon, a crowd, a robot seller, floating lanterns, big low-gravity jumps, and the Earth in the sky.

It was "super bright and colourful, like neon signs at night", pinks and blues and greens glowing against
the grey. I was at a night market on the moon, stalls in rows on the grey dusty ground, with the Earth big
and blue in the black sky above. There were lots of people in ordinary clothes, no spacesuits, and nobody
thought that was strange.

At one stall a robot was selling "noodles in glowing bowls", a tall thin silver robot with a round head. My
best friend Priya was with me and she bought two bowls. Paper lanterns were floating up from the stalls and
just kept going up into the black sky.

Every step was a huge slow jump, and Priya and I started jumping higher and higher, laughing, until we were
above the stalls with the lanterns. That's the last thing I remember.
