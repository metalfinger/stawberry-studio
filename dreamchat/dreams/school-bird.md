# The teacher who became a heron
Source: written for testing (25 Sep 2026), not a DreamBank report: a dream shaped to exercise a long corridor with a door at its end, a classroom with rows of desks, a person who turns into an animal, a view from high above, and the dreamer changing size.

I was back at my old school, walking down "a very long green corridor" with lockers on both sides, late for
an exam. At the end there was a wooden door with a small round window in it. Inside, the classroom was full
of students at rows of desks, but none of them had faces, just smooth skin. My old maths teacher, "Mrs Okafor,
in her red cardigan", was writing numbers on the blackboard.

When she turned round she had become "a tall grey heron", still wearing the red cardigan. She walked to the
window on her long legs and flew out, and somehow I was flying next to her, over the town. I could see the
school roof far below us and the river shining. I felt very small, "like a sparrow". That's where it ended.
