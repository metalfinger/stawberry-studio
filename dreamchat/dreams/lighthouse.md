# The lighthouse and the paper boats
Source: written for testing (25 Sep 2026), not a DreamBank report: a dream shaped to exercise a staircase that is climbed, a small thing held in the hand, a vehicle seen in several moments, a room at the top of a climb, and a place that turns into something else.

I was walking along a grey beach with my dog, a small brown terrier, and I was carrying "an old brass key" in
my hand. There was a white lighthouse at the end of the beach, and I knew the key was for its door. I opened
the door and climbed "a spiral staircase that went round and round", with the dog running ahead of me.

At the top there was a round room with windows all the way round, and my father was sitting at a table
folding "paper boats out of newspaper". He didn't look up. He gave me one, and when I looked out of the window
the sea had turned into "a field of tall yellow grass" with a red tractor driving slowly through it.

Then I was in the tractor's cab, next to the driver, who I didn't know, still holding the paper boat. The
tractor stopped at the edge of the field, where the beach had been, and I put the paper boat down in the
grass. That's all I remember.
