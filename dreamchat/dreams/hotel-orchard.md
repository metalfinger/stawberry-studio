# The hotel lift and the orchard
Source: written for testing (26 Sep 2026), not a DreamBank report: a dream shaped to exercise a small enclosed place that moves (a lift), a door that opens onto an outdoor place that should not be there, someone who changes age, many small things that glow, and a large animal met up close.

I was in an old hotel, in a tiny lift with "brass walls and a folding metal gate", going up with my best
friend from school, Tomas. We were adults. The lift kept going up for much too long, and when I looked at
Tomas again he was about ten years old, in the school uniform we used to wear, and he didn't seem to notice.

The lift stopped and the gate opened, and instead of a corridor there was an orchard, at dusk, with rows of
apple trees and "every apple glowing softly like a lamp". Tomas ran ahead between the trees. A white horse was
standing in the middle of the orchard, very still, and it lowered its head so I could touch its nose. It was
warm. Then Tomas was gone and I woke up.
