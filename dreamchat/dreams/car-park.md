# The car park stairs
Source: written for testing (26 Sep 2026), not a DreamBank report: a dream shaped to exercise an old black-and-white film look, a chase on stairs that never end, a dog that follows, a car that becomes a boat, and rain inside a building.

It looked "like an old black and white film, grainy and flickering". I was in a big concrete car park at
night, looking for my car, and I knew someone was following me, but every time I turned round there was only
"a thin grey dog" sitting on the ramp, watching me. I started running up the stairs, and the stairs just kept
going, floor after floor, the same number painted on every wall, "level 4, level 4, level 4".

When I finally came out on the roof it was raining, but the rain was falling upwards, from the ground into the
sky. My car was there, but it was "a little wooden rowing boat" parked in a space between the lines, with the
grey dog already sitting in it. I got in and the roof of the car park filled with water around us, and we
floated. That's where I woke up.
