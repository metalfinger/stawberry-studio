# The flooded library
Source: written for testing (26 Sep 2026), not a DreamBank report: a dream shaped to exercise a place that changes as the water rises, a big animal indoors, a small boat that two people ride in, many floating things, and someone the dreamer knows who acts strangely calm.

I was in the old city library, the big reading room with "a green glass lamp on every desk" and shelves up to
the ceiling. It was quiet and nobody else was there. Then I noticed water coming in under the doors, and it
kept rising, over the desks, until the books started floating off the shelves, "hundreds of them, open like
birds".

My older sister was suddenly there in "a little yellow rowing boat", completely calm, and she told me to get
in. I climbed in and she rowed us between the shelves. A whale swam slowly past us under the water, so big
it filled the whole aisle, and it didn't scare me at all.

We rowed up to the high round window at the top of the room. My sister opened it and outside it was morning
and the whole city was underwater, just rooftops and the tops of trees. Then I woke up.
