# The ice head
Source: Stiles #047, 18 March 1898 (DreamBank)

I was talking to a young woman, standing by the autoclave. She excused herself and walked off to
the far end of the room. When she came back she had "an irregular block of glittering ice set upon
her shoulders in place of her head." She just stood in front of me, quite naturally, not saying
anything. Then the ice started to melt and trickle. Little channels formed, the angles rounded
away, and "the sculpturing went on rapidly", until it was "a beautifully molded head of a horse;
eyes, ears, and nostrils faithfully carved out in the clearest ice." That's where it ends for me.
It didn't feel frightening; the melting felt more like carving than falling apart.
