# The Meads's house
Source: Alta #1, 1957 (DreamBank)

It was at the Meads's house, but "it's bigger inside than out". Just inside there's a European
village, "with a cobblestone street", and "a Pied-Piper sort of man with curly hair" who can do
things like juggle. I go up the back stairs (the real house doesn't have any), then straight down
the other side, because there's a second set right away. Then down "a short empty hallway that
turns a corner", where I find a tiny room. A young woman "with shoulder-length blonde hair in a
pageboy" is in there, cooking at "a stove that almost fills the room". She's nice to me.

Then I'm outside, waiting for my aunt to pick me up. She arrives in "a little round convertible"
and we go for a drive, not very far: we cross "a little bridge over a creek", then double back, and
she drops me off at the house again. Inside (I think it was inside) I sit with a couple of people,
playing with "a string of blue balloons".
