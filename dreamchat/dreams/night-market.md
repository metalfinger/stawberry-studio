# The night market and the talking fish
Source: written for testing (25 Sep 2026), not a DreamBank report: a dream shaped to exercise a dense crowd, strings of lights, a talking animal held in the hands, a bicycle ridden by two, a bridge at night, and rain that starts partway through.

I was at a night market with my older sister. It was crowded, "hundreds of people", and there were strings of
little yellow lights hanging over the stalls. My sister wanted to buy fish, so we stopped at a stall where an
old man had fish laid out on ice. One of the fish, "a silver one about as long as my arm", looked at me and
said my name. Nobody else seemed to hear it. The old man wrapped it in newspaper and gave it to me, and I held
it against my chest.

Then we were riding one bicycle, my sister pedalling and me sitting on the back holding the fish, going over
"a narrow iron bridge" above a black river. It started to rain, big warm drops, and the lights of the market
were behind us. The fish said my name again, quietly, and I woke up.
