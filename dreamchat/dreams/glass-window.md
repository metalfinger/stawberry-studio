# The glass world
Source: Chuck, journal entry 12 February 1993 (DreamBank)

After a long journey I reached my parents' house, and I was waiting there for something. "I step
into the window as if to walk through it, but when I get there I find that everything on the
other side is made of glass just like the window itself. I walk around in this glassy world where
the view is translucent but blurry. All the objects I see, including trees, grass and rocks,
radiate light through the eerie haze." The window wasn't a way out. The outside was the window.
