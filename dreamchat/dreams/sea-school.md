# The school under the sea
Source: written for testing (26 Sep 2026), not a DreamBank report: a dream shaped to exercise an underwater place in murky blue-green, swimming, fish as a crowd, a teacher who turns into an octopus, and a bell that rings as bubbles.

It was all "murky blue and green, like looking through a fish tank that needs cleaning", with light coming
down in wobbly beams from above. I was back at my primary school but the whole school was underwater, and I
could breathe fine. I swam down the corridor instead of walking.

In my old classroom the desks were covered in seaweed and the other pupils were all fish, "little silver
fish sitting at the desks". My teacher, Mr Hale, was at the front in his brown jacket, and while he was
writing on the board he slowly turned into "a huge orange octopus", still holding the chalk in one arm.

Then the school bell rang, but it came out as a big stream of bubbles going up to the surface, and all the
fish swam out of the window at once. I tried to follow them and woke up.
