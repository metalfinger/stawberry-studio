# The crayon cat
Source: written for testing (26 Sep 2026), not a DreamBank report: a dream shaped to exercise a children's crayon-drawing look, the dreamer being very small, a giant animal carrying them, a garden seen from ground level, a crowd of animals, and a house seen from above.

Everything looked "like a drawing I did in crayon when I was six", thick waxy lines and colours going over
the edges. I was tiny, as small as a mouse, standing in my mum's back garden in the grass, and the grass was
taller than me. Our old cat Biscuit, "ginger with one white ear", came through the grass and she was
enormous, as big as a bus to me.

She picked me up gently by the back of my jumper, the way cats carry kittens, and climbed the drainpipe up
onto the roof of the house. From the roof I could see all the gardens in the street, and in every garden
there was a cat sitting very still, dozens of them, "all looking up at us". Biscuit put me down on the warm
roof tiles and lay down next to me, and that was it.
