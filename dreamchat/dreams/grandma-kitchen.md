# Grandmother's kitchen in black and white
Source: written for testing (26 Sep 2026), not a DreamBank report: a dream shaped to exercise an old black-and-white film look, a small room with three people, a cake and a telephone, a room that shrinks, and the dreamer becoming a child.

The whole dream was "in black and white, like an old film with scratches on it". I was in my grandmother's
kitchen, the one from when I was little, with the checked floor and the big round clock. My grandmother was
at the table cutting a cake, and my uncle Ray was leaning in the doorway smoking.

The phone on the wall started ringing, the old kind with a long curly cord. Nobody answered it. My
grandmother kept cutting the cake, "slice after slice, but it never got smaller".

The kitchen started getting smaller around us, the walls moving in slowly, and when I looked down I was a
child again, in the red cardigan I used to wear, even though everything else was still black and white.
The phone was still ringing when I woke up.
