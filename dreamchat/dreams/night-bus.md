# The night bus and the kitchen boat
Source: written for testing (25 Sep 2026), not a DreamBank report: a dream shaped to exercise a vehicle, a held light that changes colour, a jump in place and time, a crowd, a table that becomes a boat, and a street that becomes a river.

I was on a night bus with my younger brother, going home through the rain. The bus was almost
empty, just the driver and "an old woman knitting at the back". My brother was holding "a paper
lantern with a candle inside it". I looked out of the window and the street lights were melting
into "long orange ribbons".

Then suddenly I was standing in my grandmother's kitchen, and it was morning. My grandmother was
at the stove making tea, and her hair was completely white, "whiter than I've ever seen it". My
brother was there too, still holding the lantern, but now it was glowing blue. A crowd of
neighbours, "maybe eight or ten of them", were squeezed into the doorway, watching quietly.

Then the kitchen table turned into "a small wooden boat", and my grandmother told me to get in. I
sat in the boat and it floated out of the kitchen door and back onto the street where the bus had
been, but the street was a river now. My brother waved at me from the bank, still holding the
blue lantern. That's when I woke up.
