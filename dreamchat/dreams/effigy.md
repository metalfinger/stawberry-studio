# The half-built effigy
Source: Lawrence #193, 20 November 2009 (DreamBank)

I was in my home town, but a future version of it, "flatter, wider and larger" than it really is.
In the distance there was "a gigantic carnival effigy being constructed of a tubby male
character". "It's enormous, lying on its side, half built. It must be about as high as a twenty
story building." "There's going to be a great celebration, but I don't know what about." I never
found out what the celebration was for.
